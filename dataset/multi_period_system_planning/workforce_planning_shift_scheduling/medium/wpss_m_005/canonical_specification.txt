Canonical specification for a deterministic medium-level workforce planning and shift scheduling instance.

Interpretation:
- A hospital ward must assign workers to Day, Evening, and Night shifts over a finite planning horizon.
- Every day-shift pair has a required staffing level that must be covered.
- Each worker can work at most one shift per day.
- A worker can only be assigned to shifts permitted by that worker's daily availability code.
- Each worker has a regular shift cap across the full horizon. Assignments above that cap are allowed only through overtime and incur a worker-specific overtime cost per extra shift.
- Each worker also has a maximum number of Night shifts.
- Each worker also has a maximum number of consecutive working days.
- A Night assignment on one day is incompatible with a Day assignment on the following day.
- No fairness term, no demand uncertainty, no multi-location coupling, and no back-up agency workers are included.

Decision variables:
- x[w,t,s] in {0,1}: equals 1 if worker w is assigned to shift s on day t
- o[w] >= 0: number of overtime shifts used by worker w above the regular cap

Objective:
- Minimize total assignment cost plus overtime cost:
  sum_{w,t,s} cost[w,s] * x[w,t,s] + sum_w overtime_cost[w] * o[w]

Constraints:
- Coverage: for every day t and shift s, sum_w x[w,t,s] >= demand[t,s]
- One shift per day: for every worker w and day t, sum_s x[w,t,s] <= 1
- Availability: x[w,t,s] may be 1 only if shift s is contained in the availability code for worker w on day t
- Regular workload with overtime: sum_{t,s} x[w,t,s] <= regular_cap[w] + o[w]
- Night limit: sum_t x[w,t,N] <= max_night[w]
- Consecutive-day limit: for every worker w and every rolling window of length max_consecutive[w] + 1, at most max_consecutive[w] assignments may appear in that window
- Night-to-Day rest: for every worker w and each consecutive pair of days (t, t+1), x[w,t,N] + x[w,t+1,D] <= 1
- Binary and nonnegative domains
