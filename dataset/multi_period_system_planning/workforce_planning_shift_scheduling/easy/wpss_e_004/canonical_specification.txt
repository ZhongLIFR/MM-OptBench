Canonical specification for a deterministic easy-level workforce planning and shift scheduling instance.

Interpretation:
- A single service unit must assign workers to shifts over a finite planning horizon.
- Every day-shift pair has a staffing requirement that must be covered.
- Each worker may work at most one shift per day.
- A worker may be assigned only to shifts permitted by that worker's daily availability code.
- Each assignment incurs the worker-specific and shift-specific cost listed below.
- No overtime, fairness terms, minimum-rest rules, consecutive-day limits, skill-substitution rules, or stochastic effects are included.

Sets:
- Workers W = ['W1', 'W2', 'W3', 'W4', 'W5', 'W6', 'W7']
- Days T = ['Mon', 'Tue', 'Wed', 'Thu', 'Fri', 'Sat', 'Sun']
- Shifts S = ['D', 'E', 'N']

Shift labels and times:
- D = Day = 07:00-15:00
- E = Evening = 15:00-23:00
- N = Night = 23:00-07:00

Daily staffing requirements:
- Mon: D=2, E=1, N=1
- Tue: D=2, E=1, N=1
- Wed: D=2, E=1, N=1
- Thu: D=2, E=1, N=1
- Fri: D=2, E=1, N=2
- Sat: D=2, E=1, N=2
- Sun: D=2, E=1, N=1

Worker-specific shift costs:
- W1: cost(D/E/N)=(7,10,15)
- W2: cost(D/E/N)=(8,8,99)
- W3: cost(D/E/N)=(99,9,7)
- W4: cost(D/E/N)=(9,11,13)
- W5: cost(D/E/N)=(6,12,99)
- W6: cost(D/E/N)=(99,10,8)
- W7: cost(D/E/N)=(11,11,11)

Availability code legend:
- OFF: no shift allowed
- D: only Day allowed
- E: only Evening allowed
- N: only Night allowed
- DE: Day or Evening allowed
- EN: Evening or Night allowed
- DEN: Day, Evening, or Night allowed

Worker-by-day availability codes:
- W1: Mon=DEN, Tue=DEN, Wed=DEN, Thu=DEN, Fri=DEN, Sat=DEN, Sun=DEN
- W2: Mon=DE, Tue=DE, Wed=DE, Thu=DE, Fri=DE, Sat=D, Sun=OFF
- W3: Mon=EN, Tue=EN, Wed=EN, Thu=EN, Fri=N, Sat=N, Sun=EN
- W4: Mon=DEN, Tue=DEN, Wed=DEN, Thu=DEN, Fri=DEN, Sat=DEN, Sun=DEN
- W5: Mon=D, Tue=D, Wed=OFF, Thu=D, Fri=D, Sat=DE, Sun=DE
- W6: Mon=N, Tue=EN, Wed=N, Thu=EN, Fri=N, Sat=EN, Sun=N
- W7: Mon=OFF, Tue=DEN, Wed=DEN, Thu=OFF, Fri=DEN, Sat=DEN, Sun=DEN

Decision variables:
- x[w,t,s] in {0,1}: equals 1 if worker w is assigned to shift s on day t

Objective:
- Minimize total assignment cost: sum_{w,t,s} cost[w,s] * x[w,t,s]

Constraints:
- Coverage: for every day t and shift s, sum_w x[w,t,s] >= demand[t,s]
- One shift per day: for every worker w and day t, sum_s x[w,t,s] <= 1
- Availability: x[w,t,s] may be 1 only if shift s is contained in the availability code for worker w on day t
- Binary variable domain
