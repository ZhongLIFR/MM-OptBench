Canonical specification for a deterministic medium-level multi-product lot-sizing and production planning instance.

Interpretation:
- For each product and period, a production quantity may be chosen.
- Production completed in a period is immediately available to satisfy that period's demand.
- Unsatisfied demand is not allowed; therefore inventory balance must hold in every period.
- End-of-period inventory may be carried into the next period and incurs a holding cost.
- If positive production is chosen for a product in a period, a fixed setup cost is incurred for that product-period.
- Total production across all products in a period is limited by the shared capacity of that period.

Decision variables:
- x[p,t] >= 0: production quantity of product p in period t
- I[p,t] >= 0: ending inventory of product p after period t
- y[p,t] in {0,1}: equals 1 if product p is produced in period t

Objective:
- Minimize total production cost + total inventory holding cost + total fixed setup cost.

Constraints:
- Inventory balance for every product and period: I[p,t-1] + x[p,t] = d[p,t] + I[p,t], with the given initial inventory at the start of t1.
- Setup linking for every product and period: x[p,t] <= M[p] * y[p,t].
- Shared capacity for every period: sum_p x[p,t] <= C[t].
- Nonnegativity of production and inventory, binary setup variables, and no backlogging.
