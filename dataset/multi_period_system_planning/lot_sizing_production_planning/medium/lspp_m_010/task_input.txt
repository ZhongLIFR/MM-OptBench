A manufacturing system produces multiple products over a sequence of time periods.

The accompanying figures provide all instance-specific data required for the planning problem.

The figures include:
- the production capacity available in each period,
- the complete product-by-period demand matrix,
- the unit production costs,
- the inventory holding costs,
- and the fixed setup costs.

In visual_0, rows correspond to products and columns correspond to time periods in the demand matrix, and the period columns align with the capacity bars above them. In visual_1, rows correspond to products and columns correspond to time periods in each cost matrix. Every matrix cell shows the exact numeric value for the corresponding parameter.

The production planning rules are:
- Demand for each product must be satisfied in the period in which it occurs.
- Inventory may be carried from one period to the next.
- Inventory holding cost is charged per unit kept after a period.
- Producing a product in a period incurs a fixed setup cost whenever production for that product is positive.
- Unit production costs may vary by product and period.
- Total production across all products in a period cannot exceed the available period capacity shown in visual_0.
- Backlogging is not allowed.
- Initial inventory is zero for all products.

Task:
Build a mathematical optimization model for this exact instance and provide executable solver code.
