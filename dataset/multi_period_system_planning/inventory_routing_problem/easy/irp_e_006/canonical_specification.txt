Canonical specification for a deterministic easy-level single-depot multi-period replenishment and routing instance.

Interpretation:
- A single depot replenishes customer inventories over a finite planning horizon.
- At most one identical truck may be dispatched in each period.
- If the truck is dispatched in a period, it must start at the depot, visit a subset of customers, and return to the depot in the same period.
- Travel between every pair of distinct nodes is allowed.
- Travel cost is the Euclidean distance between displayed coordinates, rounded to two decimals (euclidean_distance_rounded_to_2_decimals).
- A customer may be skipped in a period if on-hand inventory is sufficient to cover that period's demand.
- Delivering to a customer in a period requires visiting that customer in the same period.
- End-of-period inventory is carried across periods and incurs customer-specific unit holding cost.
- End-of-period inventory may not become negative and may not exceed the corresponding customer-specific inventory capacity.
- No multiple depots, split deliveries, stochastic demand, time windows, backlogging, or lost sales are included.

Decision variables:
- x[i,j,t] in {0,1}: equals 1 if the truck directly travels from node i to node j in period t
- z[c,t] in {0,1}: equals 1 if customer c is visited in period t
- q[c,t] >= 0: delivery quantity sent to customer c in period t
- I[c,t] >= 0: end-of-period inventory at customer c after period t
- y[t] in {0,1}: equals 1 if the truck is dispatched in period t
- u[c,t] >= 0: route-order helper variable for subtour elimination

Objective:
- Minimize total routing cost + total holding cost + fixed dispatch cost.

Constraints:
- Inventory balance for every customer and period.
- End-of-period inventory cannot exceed the customer-specific inventory capacity.
- Delivery quantity is zero unless the customer is visited in that period.
- Total quantity delivered in a period cannot exceed truck capacity if the truck is used.
- If the truck is used in a period, it leaves the depot once and returns once.
- Every visited customer has exactly one incoming arc and one outgoing arc in that period.
- Customer visits are not allowed when the truck is not dispatched.
- MTZ-style subtour elimination is enforced separately for each period.
- Binary and nonnegativity domains hold as defined above.
