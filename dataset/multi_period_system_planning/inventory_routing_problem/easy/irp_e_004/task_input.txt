A distribution system must plan periodic replenishment and vehicle movement using the exact data shown in the accompanying visual artifacts.

This instance uses two visual files.

visual_0.png:
- a depot-customer map showing the exact coordinates of every displayed node,
- a compact fleet panel listing the exact per-period truck capacity and fixed dispatch cost,
- and a routing-rule panel stating how travel cost is computed from the displayed coordinates.

visual_1.png:
- a customer inventory table listing the exact initial inventory, maximum storage capacity, and unit holding cost for each customer,
- a total-demand bar chart showing aggregate demand in each displayed period,
- and a customer-by-period demand heatmap whose cells contain the exact demand values.

Operational rules:
- At most one identical truck may be dispatched in each period.
- If dispatched, the truck starts at the depot, may visit a subset of customers, and returns to the depot in the same period.
- Travel between every pair of distinct nodes is allowed.
- Travel cost equals the Euclidean distance between the displayed coordinates, rounded to two decimals.
- A customer may receive inventory in a period only if the truck visits that customer in that same period.
- End-of-period inventory is carried across periods and incurs the displayed customer-specific holding cost.
- End-of-period inventory may not become negative and may not exceed the displayed customer-specific storage capacity.
- Total delivered quantity in a period may not exceed the truck capacity.
- No multiple depots, stochastic demand, backlogging, time windows, or lost sales are included.

Task:
Build a mathematical optimization model for this exact instance and provide executable solver code.
