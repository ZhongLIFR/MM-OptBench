Operational rules:
- Each generator may be either off or on in each period.

- Turning a generator on in a period incurs its startup cost if it was off in the immediately preceding period.
- The initial on/off status before the first period is: G1=off, G2=off, G3=off, G4=off.
- No ramping constraints, minimum up-time constraints, minimum down-time constraints, transmission constraints, reserve constraints, load shedding, or stochastic effects are included.
