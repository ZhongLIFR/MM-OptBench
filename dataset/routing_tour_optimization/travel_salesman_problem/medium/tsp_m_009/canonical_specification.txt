Decision variables:
- x[i,j] in {0,1}: equals 1 if the tour directly travels from city i to city j
- u[i]: visit-order variable for MTZ subtour elimination, defined for all cities except the reference city

Objective:
- Minimize total travel cost sum_{i != j} c[i,j] * x[i,j].

Constraints:
- Exactly one outgoing arc from every city.
- Exactly one incoming arc to every city.
- MTZ subtour elimination constraints over all non-reference cities.
- Binary arc variables and bounded order variables.
