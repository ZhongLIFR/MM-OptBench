Interpretation:
- A single vehicle starts at the depot, visits every customer exactly once, and returns to the depot.
- The routing graph is complete: travel between every ordered pair of distinct nodes is allowed.
- Travel times are deterministic and equal the Euclidean distances between displayed coordinates, rounded to 2 decimals.
- Service at each customer must begin within the customer-specific time window.
- If the vehicle arrives before the time window opens, it may wait.
- There are no capacities, multiple vehicles, forbidden arcs, stochastic times, or time-dependent costs.
