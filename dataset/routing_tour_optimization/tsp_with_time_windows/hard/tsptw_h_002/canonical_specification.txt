- x[i,j] in {0,1}: equals 1 if the vehicle directly travels from node i to node j

Objective:
- Minimize total travel time sum_{i != j} c[i,j] * x[i,j].

Constraints:
- Exactly one outgoing arc from every node.
- Exactly one incoming arc to every node.
- Time-propagation constraints linking consecutive visits.
- Time-window bounds at every customer node.
- The depot service-start time is fixed at 0.
- Connectivity is enforced through the time-based formulation.
