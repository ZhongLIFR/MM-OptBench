Objective:
- Minimize total fixed activation cost plus total variable travel cost across all activated vehicles.

Constraints:
- Each customer has exactly one outgoing service arc across the fleet.
- Each customer has exactly one incoming service arc across the fleet.
- For each vehicle, flow is conserved at every customer it visits.
- Depot departure and depot return are each equal to the vehicle-activation variable.
- The total demand assigned to a vehicle cannot exceed that vehicle's capacity.
- Vehicle-specific MTZ-style constraints eliminate subtours and enforce capacity-consistent route order.
- Arc and activation variables are binary; load/helper variables are continuous.
