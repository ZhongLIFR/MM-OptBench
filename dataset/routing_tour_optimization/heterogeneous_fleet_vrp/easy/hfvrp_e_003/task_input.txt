A delivery-planning instance is specified by a single accompanying visual artifact.

The visual jointly provides all instance-specific numerical data required for the optimization problem.

The visual includes:
- the depot location,
- the labeled position of every customer in a two-dimensional map,
- the exact coordinate pair and demand value associated with each customer, written directly on the map next to that customer,
- the exact coordinate pair for the depot,
- a fleet table describing the available vehicle categories, vehicle counts, capacities, fixed activation costs, and distance multipliers,
- and the exact rule used to convert coordinates into travel cost.

Interpretation:
- Each activated vehicle starts from the depot, serves a subset of customers, and returns to the depot.
- Each customer must be visited exactly once by exactly one activated vehicle.
- The routing graph is complete: travel between every pair of distinct nodes is allowed.
- Each available vehicle has its own capacity, fixed activation cost, and per-distance multiplier inherited from its category.
- If a vehicle is activated, its fixed activation cost is incurred exactly once.
- Variable travel cost equals the vehicle-specific distance multiplier times the base distance defined in the visual.
- The total demand assigned to a vehicle must not exceed that vehicle's capacity.
- There are no time windows, multiple depots, split deliveries, stochastic demands, or customer-vehicle incompatibilities.

Task:
1) Write a mathematical optimization model for this exact instance.
2) Provide solver-executable code implementing the model.

How to read the visual:
- The left panel shows the depot and customer locations on a coordinate plane.
- Each colored customer annotation on the map gives that customer's exact demand and coordinates.
- The depot label on the map also shows the depot's exact coordinates.
- The upper-right table lists each vehicle category's available count, capacity, fixed activation cost, and per-distance multiplier.
- The lower-right box states the exact base-distance and travel-cost rules.

Output requirement:
- Construct an exact optimization model for the instance encoded by the visual.
- Use the depot, customer, fleet, demand, coordinate, and travel-cost information shown in the figure.
- Do not introduce assumptions that are not supported by the visual artifact.
