Canonical specification for a deterministic single-depot Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem instance.

Interpretation:
- A fleet of identical vehicles starts at the depot, serves customers, and returns to the depot.
- Each customer must be visited exactly once by exactly one vehicle.
- Travel between every pair of distinct nodes is allowed, so the routing graph is complete.
- Vehicle capacity is binding and the total demand served on each route must not exceed the common vehicle capacity.
- There are no time windows, multiple depots, forbidden arcs, stochastic demands, or heterogeneous vehicle capacities.
- The optimization goal is to minimize total routing cost across all used vehicles.

Decision variables:
- x[i,j,v] in {0,1}: equals 1 if vehicle v directly travels from node i to node j
- u[i]: cumulative load/order helper variable for customer i used in MTZ-style capacity subtour elimination

Objective:
- Minimize total routing cost sum_v sum_{i != j} c[i,j] * x[i,j,v] plus optional fleet fixed costs when present.

Constraints:
- Every customer has exactly one outgoing service arc across the fleet.
- Every customer has exactly one incoming service arc across the fleet.
- For each vehicle, flow is conserved at every node it visits.
- Each vehicle can leave the depot at most once and return at most once.
- The total demand served by each vehicle does not exceed the common capacity.
- MTZ-style load constraints eliminate subtours and enforce capacity-consistent route order.
- Arc variables are binary and load/order variables are continuous.
