Canonical specification for a deterministic 0-1 multi-dimensional knapsack instance.

Interpretation:
- Each candidate item may either be selected or not selected.
- Selecting item i contributes its item-specific value.
- Selecting item i consumes resource in every displayed resource dimension according to the instance-specific coefficients.
- For each resource dimension, total consumption of selected items must not exceed the corresponding resource limit.
- There are no fractional selections, logical dependencies between items, stochastic parameters, or time-indexed extensions.
- The optimization goal is to maximize the total value of selected items.
