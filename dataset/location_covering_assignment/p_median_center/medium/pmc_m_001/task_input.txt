A spatial service-planning visual is provided.

The visual is a single map:
- Candidate service sites and demand points are shown on the same spatial layout.
- Candidate sites are shown as squares.
- Demand points are shown as circles.
- The same-colored boxes attached below the nodes report each node's public attributes.
- The node-attached boxes provide the exact coordinates and, when relevant, the demand weights.

Interpretation:
- Exactly 3 candidate sites must be selected.
- Each demand point must be assigned to exactly one selected site.
- Assignment distance is the Euclidean distance implied by the displayed coordinates.
- The demand weights multiply the corresponding assignment distances in the objective.
- There are no capacities, fixed opening costs, assignment prohibitions, multiple periods, or stochastic elements.

Task:
1) Write a mathematical optimization model for this exact instance.
2) Provide solver-executable code implementing the model.

Objective:
Minimize total weighted assignment distance.
