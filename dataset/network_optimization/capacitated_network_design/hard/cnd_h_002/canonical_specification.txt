Problem statement
-----------------
Choose which candidate arcs to install and how to route the required
shipment from node 1 to node 15.

If arc e = (i,j) is installed:
  - pay its fixed installation cost once;
  - allow at most its capacity units of flow on that arc.

If x units are sent on an installed arc:
  - pay its variable routing cost times x.

Flow may split across several installed routes, but total outgoing flow
from the source and total incoming flow to the sink must match the
required shipment exactly.

Objective:
  minimize total fixed installation cost plus total variable routing
  cost.
